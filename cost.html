US 287, I-35W to Rhome: the cost of deferred capacity

US 81/287 between I-35W and Rhome is a four-lane freeway carrying up to 81,000 vehicles a day, 22% of them trucks. TxDOT has designed the fix: three main-lane widening projects totaling $589.6 million. None is in TxDOT's 10-year Unified Transportation Program (UTP). All three are listed as "Planning, 10+ years", with estimated letting in 2035.

This assessment estimates what the corridor costs travelers, freight carriers and the public each year while that capacity is deferred. It combines two measures. Crash costs use TxDOT crash records and the Federal Highway Administration's 2024 cost per crash by severity. Congestion costs use the Texas A&M Transportation Institute's (TTI) measured travel delay.

Independent analysis by a 76052 resident. Crash data comes from TxDOT's Crash Records Information System (CRIS). Programmed work vs. deferred capacity

TxDOT's Project Tracker lists nine US 81/287 projects in the corridor. The frontage-road, ramp, interchange and intersection work ($355.7M) is in the UTP, with lettings from 2025 to 2030. The three projects that add main lanes ($589.6M) are not. Until they are built, the main lanes stay at two per direction.

Congestion cost: measured delay

TTI's statewide congestion analysis, the basis of TxDOT's annual "100 Most Congested Roadways" list, measures delay on every major segment from GPS speed data. On US 81/287 from I-35W to Avondale-Haslet Rd, the annual cost of delay rose from $1.05 million in 2019 to $8.0 million in 2024. The 95th-percentile planning time index rose from 1.03 to 1.44. A trip that takes 10 minutes in free flow now needs 14.4 minutes of buffer to arrive on time.

Cost of waiting until 2035

If crash and congestion costs simply hold at their 2023–25 average, with no further growth, the corridor accumulates the costs below between 2026 and the estimated 2035 letting. On a comprehensive basis, the cumulative cost passes the $589.6M widening estimate during 2032. On an economic-only basis it reaches about $238M by 2035.

Findings and limitations

Findings

  • The main-lane widening of US 81/287 from I-35W to Rhome ($589.6M across three projects) is not programmed in the UTP; TxDOT lists estimated letting in 2035.
  • Measured congestion cost on the I-35W to Avondale-Haslet segment rose 7.6× from 2019 to 2024, to $8.0M a year.
  • Crash costs on the corridor averaged $87.4M a year (comprehensive) or $15.6M a year (economic) in 2023–25.
  • At zero growth, comprehensive corridor costs reach the widening estimate by about 2032, three years before the earliest estimated letting.

Limitations

  • Crash costs value all reported crashes. Not every crash is attributable to roadway capacity, and this assessment does not estimate how many a widening would prevent.
  • Comprehensive costs include quality-of-life valuations; economic costs are shown alongside for a narrower, cash-based view.
  • TTI congestion costs cover recurring and non-recurring delay measured from INRIX speed data. They exclude freight-reliability and logistics costs beyond travel time and fuel.
  • Project costs are TxDOT construction estimates and exclude right-of-way, engineering and inflation to the letting year.

Question for TxDOT and the RTC

The frontage-road and interchange work now programmed will not add main-lane capacity. With corridor costs on the order of $96M a year on a comprehensive basis, residents need to know what it would take to move the three main-lane projects (CSJs 0014-15-037, 0014-15-076 and 0013-08-111) into the UTP before 2035.

Methodology

Crashes. TxDOT CRIS records for January 1, 2016 through September 25, 2026, combining a US 81/US 287 highway query (Tarrant and Wise counties) with the 287/114/35W corridor map query, de-duplicated by Crash ID. Kept: crashes on US 81/287 main lanes or frontage roads between the I-35W split (32.885° N) and the north end of Rhome (33.08° N). Each crash is valued at FHWA's 2024 unit cost for its most severe injury level: K $15,988,000; A $1,705,100; B $384,000; C $204,600; O (property damage only) $18,100 comprehensive; economic values K $2,238,500; A $272,700; B $80,800; C $53,000; O $18,100.

Congestion. TTI 2025 Texas Top 100 Congested Road Segments, full statewide list (2,155 segments), data years 2019 and 2021–2024. Segment 8000216 (US 81/US 287, Avondale Haslet Rd/FM 718 to North Fwy/IH 35W) and 8000215 (Illinois St/FM 407 to Avondale Haslet Rd/FM 718).

Projects. TxDOT Project Tracker, all US 81/287 projects intersecting the corridor.

Data limitations

  • CRIS records without coordinates cannot be placed and are excluded, so crash counts and costs are slightly understated.
  • The northern TTI segment extends beyond Rhome to FM 407; its delay is negligible either way.
  • FHWA unit costs are national values in 2024 dollars; Texas-specific costs may differ.
  • Fatal crashes are rare events, so annual comprehensive costs fluctuate widely. Multi-year averages are used for projections.
